# Metalloprotein project 2020

Since the student hadn't decided on the protein to simulate as I prepare the first part of demo session, I used PDE as the example.

For the hands-on exercise using the student's own laptop, a small system of model zinc finger (PDB: 1ZNF (A)) was selected. This allowed the simulation to be finished within a reasonable amont of time. 

An additional simulation of a larger metalloprotein system had been prepared by the student and ran on a better computer in lab.

Trajectories are not provided due to the large filesize.

This was a 2-semester (12-month) individual project. The project included
- literature review, 
- two progress reports and one final thesis required by the department 
- research planning, 
- bioinformatic analysis of metalloproteins on the RCSB PDB,
- experimental design and hands-on experience on running MD simulation.
