# FYP2020

In 2020, I am in charge of 3 undergraduate students who worked on projects related to molecular modelling, and 3 work on other projects.

One student was under the supervision of my superviosor (computational chemist). He/she worked on the metalloprotein 2-semester project.

2 students came from two different experimental groups (molecular/cell biologist and structural biologist/crystallographer). They were interested to apply both experimental and computational techniques in their research, hence being referred here by their superviosors.

Slides adjusted according to the lesson each superviosor would like to delivered to their students, and based on the superviosor's comments on the student.
