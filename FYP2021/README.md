# FYP2021

In this year I am only in charge of one undergraduate research student.

The hands-on docking training session was also prepared for research postgraduate students and RA from the group of our experimental collaborator. 

The small group of people who attended the session included 2 Windows 10 users and 4 MacOS users. Since ADT and related tools were not compatible with recent version of MacOS at the time of the demo, we used the UCSF Chimera-Vina interface.
